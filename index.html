How the factory works

  1. Each day an issue becomes a spec, and a clean-context builder implements it from the spec alone.
  2. Adversarial critics must fail to reject the build — every must-pass rubric line, plus sign-off from 2 of 3 critics.
  3. It deploys the same day with a live demo, and this page updates itself from the factory dashboard.
  4. The doctrine and every sign-off are public in factory-hub.
